Kirana Store – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · App ID: com.ivsntejesh.kiranastore

Kirana Store ("the app") lets customers order groceries from a local kirana store, and lets the store owner manage items and orders. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. The app is published by ivsntejesh ("we", "us").

Information we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Name and email addressWhen you sign up with emailTo create and secure your account and identify your orders
Phone numberWhen you log in with a phone OTP, or enter it for deliveryTo verify your login, and so the store can contact you about deliveries
Delivery address (house/street, city, pincode)At checkout or in ProfileTo deliver your order; saved so you don't retype it
Order history (items, quantities, prices, totals, status)When you place, or the store updates, an orderTo fulfil orders and show your order history
Shop details (shop name and village)If you ask for a shop account to buy for your shopSo the store can approve the account, show the shop on your orders and plan its delivery route
Credit (udhaar) records: amounts bought on credit, payments and the balanceIf the store gives you goods on creditSo you and the store can both see what is owed
Shopping lists you type, paste or share into the appWhen you use "Order from your list"To find the items for your cart. The list is read on your phone and is not stored
App usage and device information (for example app opens, screens viewed, device model, OS version, and an app-instance identifier)Automatically, via Google Analytics for FirebaseTo understand how the app is used and improve it

Your shopping cart is stored only on your device and is cleared when you log out. The app does not collect your location, contacts, or payment card or bank details. Payment is made to the store in cash (on delivery or at pick-up), or later if the store gives you credit.

Voice: if you choose "Say it", your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's) turns what you say into text and gives the app only that text. The app does not record or store audio. Your speech is handled under that service's own privacy terms.

Store owners can upload product photos. These are pictures of products, not personal information.

How information is stored and shared

Information is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Google Analytics for Firebase), which processes it on our behalf. Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests come from the genuine app, to protect the store from abuse. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). See Firebase's privacy information.

Your name, phone number, delivery address, shop details, order details and credit records are visible to the store owner so they can fulfil your orders and keep accounts. If you choose to, the store may send you your bill on WhatsApp. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login, profile, saved delivery address and shop details, and cancels any pending orders.

Without the app: email ivsntejesh@gmail.com from the email address on your account (or include the phone number you log in with) with the subject "Delete my Kirana Store account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept: records of orders you already placed (items, amounts, dates and the delivery details on those orders) and any credit (udhaar) records, including an unpaid balance, are kept by the store only as long as needed for its accounting, tax and legal obligations, and are then deleted. Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting in Google Analytics (by default 2 months, up to 14 months) and is not linked to your name or email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
